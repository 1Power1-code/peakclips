Privacy Policy – PeakClips Uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026

PeakClips Uploader is used only by the operator of the YouTube channel PeakClips Daily to upload videos to that channel and read its own statistics.

  • The app accesses YouTube data of the operator's own channel only (upload videos, read channel and video information, read YouTube Analytics reports).
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • No data of other YouTube users is collected or stored, except public comments on the channel's own videos, which are read to handle removal requests.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Contact: via the YouTube channel @peakclipsdotgg.
